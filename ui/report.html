        How FirstTrace researches a company
        1. Discover. Every UnternehmerTUM prototype grant and every BayStartUP business plan roster linked from the organizer’s news index (nominees, phase winners, finalists, winners).
        2. Find the website. Analyst-verified domains first. Otherwise FirstTrace tries the company name as a domain and only accepts it if the site’s legal notice names the company and a matching city or German address.
        3. Read the legal notice. German law (§ 5 DDG) requires an Impressum with the legal entity, address, register court and number, and the people who represent the company. FirstTrace extracts them and flags teams that are not incorporated yet.
        4. Read the website. Pilot, beta, demo, pricing, customers, hiring, named investors or investor pages, with the exact quote.
        5. Date the footprint. First Internet Archive capture of the site and first Hacker News post linking it. Archive dates are labeled as such.
        6. Score and remember. HTGF readiness from eight sourced checks, the investment window, and a diff against the previous scan.
        More sources to watch
        • EXIST Startup Grant · Early academic teams. Look for dated award announcements from their universities.
        • The Reveal · Official demo-day roster. Check each exhibitor’s funding separately.
        • CORDIS · EU research awards. Look for German ERC Proof of Concept projects, then verify spinout intent.

        FirstTrace surfaces public evidence. Funding, identity and first visibility stay unverified until an analyst confirms them. FirstTrace drafts; the analyst decides whether to reach out.
