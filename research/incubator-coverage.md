Programme affiliation and legally documented operation in Germany are separate diligence facts.

## Founder signals

The parser looks for explicit prior employment or earlier startups in public biographies with an unambiguous named founder. Evidence shows the source and the attributed text. The watch criterion **Founder track record** detects newly captured live evidence. Career highlights are searchable under **Funding, sector and review filters** and visible in **People & diligence**. Reviewed source notes can supply additional context, for example the documented link between Simra's Holger Niessner and the prior venture Tacit. That example is reviewed evidence.

Staff, colleagues and advisors need separate attribution. Employer prestige contributes no points on its own, and founder history often remains unknown. For wider coverage, the next source should be a permitted, searchable profile or launch feed with independently linkable bios and dates. This is where to test a hypothesis about alumni without treating everyone leaving a large employer as a founder.
