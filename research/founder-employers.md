# Former employers used in FirstTrace

The [shared employer catalogue](../founder_employers.py) powers the LinkedIn scouting presets and the prior-employer detector. It has five groups for German startup alumni, European and Israeli startup alumni, global tech and AI, finance and consulting, and industry and deep tech.

- **German and European founder networks.** The 2023 Accel–Dealroom sample recorded 38 startups from Delivery Hero alumni, 37 from Zalando alumni, 31 from N26 alumni and 14 from Celonis alumni. The same sample identified alumni-founded startups from Criteo, Glovo, Klarna, Spotify, Revolut, Wise, Monzo and others. The 2025 edition highlights Klarna, Spotify and Zalando at the top of its European and Israeli research cohort. [2023 report, pages 8–9](https://dealroom.co/uploaded/2023/12/Accel-x-Dealroom-founder-factory-report-Dec-2023.pdf) · [2025 findings](https://www.accel.com/news/report-how-europe-and-israels-unicorns-have-helped-drive-growth-of-regions-tech-ecosystem)
- **Big Tech and AI.** The 2026 study identifies Alphabet and Microsoft as the largest Big Tech employer sources among the newer unicorn founders it studied. The narrower 2024 GenAI study identifies Google, DeepMind, Facebook, Amazon, Intel, Apple and McKinsey among the previous affiliations it tracked. [2026 report](https://dealroom.co/reports/europe-israels-founder-factories-in-the-age-of-ai-2026) · [GenAI report, pages 10–11](https://dealroom.co/uploaded/2024/06/Dealroom-Accel-GenAI-Report-June-2024.pdf)
- **Finance and industrial scouting.** The catalogue also includes documented examples such as PayPal and analyst-selected employers in markets relevant to HTGF, including Bosch, ZEISS, Infineon, Siemens Energy, ASML, industry labs, banks and trading firms. Keep these entries as search hypotheses. The groups have no shared comparison baseline. [Dealroom founder-factory study](https://dealroom.co/blog/founder-factories-fuelling-next-gen-startups/) · [HTGF investment focus](https://www.htgf.de/en/page/61/)

LinkedIn searches use a selected employer and a short set of founding keywords. The detector recognizes aliases including Facebook/Meta and TransferWise/Wise. It checks TUM biographies, named founders in linked GitHub READMEs, and scout-supplied LinkedIn notes.
It requires explicit wording attributed to a named person. A mention of a customer, university or partner is insufficient. Employer history can inform diligence when relevant work is documented. The company name itself carries zero scoring points.
