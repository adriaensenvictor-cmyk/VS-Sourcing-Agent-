# HTGF research and product decision

## Official challenge and the analyst's problem

MAIN Challenge Track pages 21–22 require a functioning sourcing engine that surfaces real leads and one standout lead with the signals and why to take a closer look. The HTGF one-pager gives five judging criteria: lead quality, earliness, creativity of signals, technical execution and scalability. It advises starting narrow, focusing on Germany, investigating who is building and filtering hobby projects, service agencies and assignments. BuilderBase page 10 controls hand-in. Page 11 gives resource locations.

HTGF publicly describes initial pre-seed and seed investments around a sweet spot of €800,000+, and lists a maximum company age of three years for its early-stage criteria. Its stated geography is a German HQ or base of operations. It invests across digital, industrial and life sciences/chemistry tracks. These criteria help prioritize a possible meeting. The hackathon brief also allows meaningful leads at the project stage. [HTGF investment criteria](https://www.htgf.de/en/)

Its Digital Tech team explicitly targets developer tools, AI/ML, enterprise software and cloud infrastructure. It looks for technical depth, business value and strong technical teams. Its corporate network could be relevant for customer introductions and pilot validation. The active-sourcing team supports investment managers with discovery and market research. The product should therefore shorten the time to an informed outreach decision. [Digital Tech](https://www.htgf.de/en/digital-tech/) · [Portfolio](https://www.htgf.de/en/portfolio/) · [Active sourcing work](https://www.htgf.de/career-2/young-talents/)

Two patterns validate the underlying thesis. Graylog2 launched in 2010, TORCH was founded by its creators in 2012, and HTGF announced seed financing in November 2013. That historical example shows an open-source project can precede its commercial company. Separately, HTGF says it encountered JeNaCell through EXIST judging in 2010, then backed the seed round in 2012. Campus signals can already matter to HTGF, so the value is systematically connecting them to proof of execution. Both cases were selected with hindsight and cannot establish the engine's hit rate or prospective lead time. [Graylog2 seed announcement](https://www.htgf.de/en/torch-secures-seed-funding-for-graylog2/) · [JeNaCell history](https://www.htgf.de/en/htgf-exit-jenacell/)

## Three concepts we considered

| Concept | Early signal | Feasibility and blind spots | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Campus project × shipped software | Prototype grant directory or company-authored university collaboration combined with a dated release | Two public source families with links. Identity resolution requires a verified org link. Undated cohort cards cannot establish historical visibility. | Ship first |
| Paper → spinout | Authors publish code, then transition to an independent project and a commercialization event | Good fit for deep tech. Multiple same-name matches, limited historical founder context and low probability of a reliable join during the sprint. | Test after MVP |
| Public event before the announcement | A niche pitch roster names a team ahead of a financing announcement | Organizers provide team and timing. Some teams already raised, and event archives or consistent machine-readable listings can be sparse. | Add one local event as a third adapter only if it improves the lead |

**FirstTrace** starts with Munich B2B software, infrastructure and devtools. It asks which teams have progressed from an idea to something an external user can obtain. The acquisition signal is a university listing. The proof is a release plus credible founder and product evidence. The original extension is to watch public company-authored university project postings for early engineering hiring or pilot intent. A university listing for a founder-created venture and a student assignment are different facts. Test the difference on real records.

- Discovery uses [UnternehmerTUM grant recipients](https://funding.unternehmertum.de/supportedteams-2/). This is an equity-free grant programme that can support projects before incorporation. Cohort presence does not establish current ownership, publication dates or a company's current headquarters. [Programme terms](https://funding.unternehmertum.de/prototyping-grants)
- The release signal uses [GitHub release metadata](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/releases/releases). Use published dates. A repository's creation timestamp does not establish when it became public. Declare any pagination bound.
- Verify the candidate's affiliation through an institution or the company, and require an explicit project–organization link before automatic release enrichment. Avoid matching solely on names. Distinguish partner endorsement, actual adoption and vendor self-report.
- Funding is an analyst review. Search the legal name and aliases across the company's site, investor announcements and a named database. List sources checked. If a dated comparison point is available, compute its date minus the earliest eligible public signal. A company launch date, grant date or our observation timestamp cannot stand in for a database entry's publication date.

## Tool choices

- GitHub is the shared source of truth. Codex can use AGENTS.md for repository instructions. Claude and agents without checkout access should be given README, AGENTS.md and the specific workstream file. Use the GitHub connector in PR review where available. The [OpenAI Codex guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/gpt-5/codex_prompting_guide) and [plugin architecture](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/concepts/plugins) describe the repository context and integration model.
- OpenAI [Web Search](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-web-search) can corroborate short-listed leads with citations. [Structured Outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs) can format extracted facts, but every material field still needs a verified source and missing values must stay missing. Test one call using BuilderBase Resources access. OpenAI also describes API credits for eligible linked subscriptions. Check the API organization before relying on capacity. [API credits](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/chatgpt-api-credits)
- OpenAI's [Codex app tooling](https://openai.com/index/codex-for-almost-everything/) and the [Product Design workflow](https://academy.openai.com/public/clubs/champions-ecqup/videos/workflow-clip-streamline-team-engagement-with-codex-2026-07-09) can accelerate one-pass review of the lead screen. Use a browser capture and annotate one thing the analyst should notice within five seconds. The demo itself can run without these tools.
- n8n can schedule a bounded scan and route newly actionable leads to an internal review queue after the team deploys the collector. ElevenLabs can turn one approved lead memo into a brief audio excerpt if it helps presentation and can be tested within 30 minutes. Keep the judging-critical evidence visible in the UI. ## Devil's advocate findings

- **“This is just a list of grants.”** Demonstrate a second source and an exclusion. The MVP's join is analyst-confirmed. Show exactly which part runs automatically and measure minutes it saves.
- **“A release could be a hobby project or an abandoned pivot.”** Require a credible team and current product direction for the best lead and ask a specific commercial diligence question. Stars are neither a team signal nor pipeline revenue.
- **“You found it early only because you already knew the company.”** The live cohort generates the names. The strongest initial row benefits from manual enrichment. Report its exact release date and why precedence remains unknown. Exclude already funded names such as Nuno when the startup is used to demonstrate new-lead selection.
- **“You can claim unknown on everything.”** Ask a reviewer to inspect the top five without seeing scores. Count independently meet-worthy leads, unknown funding states and identity failures. If the best claim fails, replace the pitch lead or present it as a prospect requiring funding verification.
- **“This will break as soon as the source changes.”** Use bounded fetching, preserve failures in the UI, add one small captured replay and watch parse counts on recurring runs. Add sources only when one extra source produces an actionable lead in the same geography.
