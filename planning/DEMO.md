# Demo and hand-in

## Best find from the initial scan

**Bevel Software, Munich.** The UnternehmerTUM grant recipient listing surfaced Bevel. TUM's collaboration page names Ali Raza, Juan Ignacio Viera Garcia and Razvan Radulescu, and describes the original legacy-code project. GitHub records a public Bevel language-server release on **19 April 2025**, direct evidence the team shipped an installable artifact. The current company site describes a Git-backed control plane for enterprise AI agents and links its open-source work. This documents a team and two product directions. Validate whether the current offering has paid deployments before making any traction claim.

**Why HTGF should take a look.** The team has shipped software and is building around the enterprise problem of managing agent tooling and access across vendors. That sits in HTGF's Digital Tech focus. The first conversation should distinguish production use from evaluation and identify who within an enterprise owns budget for governance infrastructure.

**Financing and earliness.** The reviewed Dealroom result lists a 2024 spinout with an undated listing and no confirmed equity round in the displayed table. The company names CDTM and UnternehmerTUM as supporters. Neither source settles the first equity round or when Dealroom made its record public. Public talks and a current database entry mean Bevel is no longer a stealth lead. Use it as a prospect with an early documented product trace, pending financing verification. Any candidate independently verified as pre-equity and more recently surfaced takes priority in the final recording.

Sources: [UnternehmerTUM](https://funding.unternehmertum.de/supportedteams-2/) · [TUM project and founders](https://www.tumcso.com/teaching/final-theses-and-project-studies-bachelor-master/available-topics/scale-with-cdtm-startup-bevel-with-idp-project) · [April 2025 release](https://github.com/Bevel-Software/bevel-language-server-releases/releases/tag/bevel-0.6.0) · [Current product](https://www.bevel.software/about) · [Dealroom baseline check](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/bevel_3)

**Exclusion to show.** The directory contains Nuno under a different spelling. A public database reports a June 2026 seed round, while the other database shows grant/spinout entries. The appropriate sourcing decision is to hold it for reconciliation. The name change also demonstrates why a simple string match does not establish identity. [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/nuno-labs/financials) · [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/nuno_labs)

**Alternative lead.** Patalyze offers software for mapping products against patents. Its founder has public TUM data engineering experience, and the product has a public technical interface. Public sources reviewed do not settle its equity status. This is a diligence prospect if the team wants a narrower enterprise use case. [Founder](https://daniel-stoll.com/) · [Product docs](https://patalyze.com/docs/mcp) · [Database](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/patalyze)

**Historical comparison.** BayStartUP listed Valuemize on 23 February 2024. Coverage of its pre-seed round appeared on 16 July 2026, 874 days later. The report computes this interval between two public sources added by an analyst. It is selected retrospectively and measures neither the timing of a database entry nor FirstTrace's actual discovery advantage.
